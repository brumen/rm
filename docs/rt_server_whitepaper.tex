\begin{minipage}[t]{0.58\textwidth}
	\section*{\textcolor{brandblue}{Executive Summary}}
	\texttt{rt\_server} is an internal real-time pricing and risk platform designed to deliver fast, consistent portfolio analytics for trading and investment workflows. It provides a shared processing layer between upstream trade and market events and downstream consumers of valuation and risk outputs.

	\vspace{0.8em}

	From a business perspective, the platform improves intraday visibility, reduces operational fragmentation across analytics workflows, and provides a scalable foundation for expanding pricing and risk capabilities across products and teams.

	\subsection*{Highlights}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Real-time portfolio analytics platform for internal users and services
		\item Shared processing layer across pricing and risk workflows
		\item Supports faster intraday visibility and downstream standardization
		\item Designed as a reusable internal capability rather than a point solution
	\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}

		\vspace{0.6em}

		\texttt{rt\_server} can be positioned as a shared analytics utility: upstream systems provide event updates, the platform transforms those updates into portfolio-level outputs, and downstream users or services consume the results for monitoring, reporting, and further analysis.

% --- BUSINESS PURPOSE ---
\section*{\textcolor{brandblue}{Business Purpose}}

The primary purpose of \texttt{rt\_server} is to shorten the time between an input event and an updated analytical view of portfolio value or risk. In practice, this supports better intraday awareness, more responsive downstream processes, and reduced dependence on fragmented point solutions.

The platform also provides a common foundation for extending analytics over time. Rather than building separate pipelines for each new output or workflow, additional capabilities can be added on top of the same runtime and distribution model.

\vspace{1.6em}

% --- VALUE & USERS ---
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\textwidth}
	\section*{\textcolor{brandblue}{Business Value}}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item \textbf{Faster visibility:} supports near-real-time refresh of portfolio analytics as positions or market conditions change.
		\item \textbf{Consistency across workflows:} creates a common processing layer for valuation and risk outputs used by multiple consumers.
		\item \textbf{Operational simplification:} reduces the need for duplicate downstream aggregation and bespoke integrations.
		\item \textbf{Scalable platform model:} provides a reusable base for adding new analytics, products, or reporting use cases over time.
		\item \textbf{Improved control posture:} creates a clearer basis for governance, monitoring, and production operating discipline.
	\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\textwidth}
	\section*{\textcolor{brandblue}{Who It Serves}}
	\texttt{rt\_server} is relevant for trading and portfolio management teams that need timely portfolio views, as well as for engineering and platform teams responsible for distributing analytics to downstream systems.

	\vspace{0.8em}

	It is also useful for stakeholders who rely on standardized pricing and risk outputs for reporting, controls, or operational monitoring.

	\section*{\textcolor{brandblue}{Representative Use Cases}}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item \textbf{Intraday portfolio monitoring} for changing exposures and valuation movements.
		\item \textbf{Standardized downstream analytics feeds} for dashboards, reporting tools, and monitoring processes.
		\item \textbf{Strategy or desk-specific extensions} built on top of a common internal platform.
		\item \textbf{Incremental platform expansion} as additional asset classes, analytics, or consumers are onboarded.

\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\textwidth}
	\section*{\textcolor{brandblue}{Operating Model}}
	\texttt{rt\_server} acts as an internal processing layer between upstream event sources and downstream analytics consumers. This allows teams to remain loosely coupled: producers of trades and market updates do not need to be tightly bound to each individual consumer of portfolio results.

	\vspace{0.8em}

	From a business and platform perspective, this model improves reuse of shared infrastructure, supports cleaner integration boundaries, and makes future expansion easier than maintaining multiple tightly connected point-to-point solutions.
\end{minipage}

\vspace{1.8em}

% --- GOVERNANCE ---
\section*{\textcolor{brandblue}{Governance Considerations}}

As the platform evolves, the main business-facing control priorities include message and data contract management, operational monitoring, traceability of outputs, restart and recovery procedures, and environment-specific deployment discipline. These areas are important for moving from a technically effective runtime toward a more mature production platform.

\vspace{1.8em}

% --- CALL OUT BOX ---
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=brandblue, coltext=white, fonttitle=\bfseries, title=Current Position and Next Steps, arc=2mm]
\texttt{rt\_server} should be viewed as a reusable internal capability for real-time valuation and risk distribution rather than as a one-off technical component. Its strategic value comes from standardizing how analytics are produced and delivered while preserving flexibility for product-specific extensions.

\vspace{0.8em}

Near-term priorities should focus on formalizing data contracts, improving observability, strengthening production controls, and expanding the set of supported analytics and business use cases.
\end{tcolorbox}
